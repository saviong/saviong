# 💫 About Me

<b>🌐 Socials:</b>

I build things that take manual work off people's desks: Power BI dashboards, Power Automate and VBA workflows, and reproducible cloud infrastructure in Terraform and Bicep.
 
The route here wasn't a straight line. I studied Chemistry in Hong Kong, and an exchange semester at TUM in Munich changed how I thought about problems and about where I wanted to work. When I started automating my own team's repetitive tasks with VBA, then rebuilt those workflows in Power Automate and saved us hours a week, the direction became obvious. I've been moving toward data and infrastructure ever since.
 
- **Now:** Laboratory Asset Coordinator at Anthony Nolan, London — built into a Power BI forecasting dashboard, automated 5+ workflows saving ~10 hours a week, and cut procurement costs ~10% through data-led ordering
- **Learning:** Deepening SQL and Python for analytics work, alongside Kubernetes
- **Ask me about:** Power Automate, Terraform, Bicep, Azure, or moving from a lab bench into tech
- **Certified:** AZ-104 (self-studied in two months, 2025) and HashiCorp Terraform Associate (earned within a month of starting)
